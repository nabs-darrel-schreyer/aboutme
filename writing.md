## Management Bandwidth: Five Early-Warning Metrics That Show You're the Bottleneck

**LinkedIn | December 2025**

This article treats management bandwidth as an operational constraint and sets out five early-warning metrics for routine agile software delivery decisions: iterations to understanding, mean time to decision, number of reminders, frequency of broken commitments, and quality debt accrual rate. Each metric is scoped to day-to-day work the manager already controls, with thresholds that mark when they have become the bottleneck, and a private sprint tally so two or more yellow weeks prompt offloading before the team has to raise it.

## From Mills's Copilot to GitHub Copilot: A 1970s Idea in My Day-to-Day .NET Work

**LinkedIn | August 2025**

Harlan Mills's "copilot" in the surgical team (as Fred Brooks told it) was a second skilled programmer supporting the lead developer - not a luxury most teams could afford. This article connects that 1970s idea to GitHub Copilot in modern .NET work: comparing OAuth approaches, driving consistent package updates across repos, and using MCP for SDLC tasks so the developer in the chair stays fully responsible while shipping faster.

## Core Capabilities for an Effective AI Implementation

**A leadership guide to evolve your organisation from data to intelligence**

Co-authored with Amanda Schreyer.

This whitepaper outlines seven core capabilities that turn static data into dynamic intelligence: data and content curation, AI-enhanced search and retrieval, intelligent matching and recommendation, context-aware data entry, AI-driven process automation, continuous learning and governance, and amplifying talent. It is written for leaders who want to scale AI responsibly without chasing models for their own sake.
