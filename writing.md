# Writing

Thought leadership and co-authored work on AI, delivery, and organisational capability.

## Core Capabilities for an Effective AI Implementation

**A leadership guide to evolve your organisation from data to intelligence**

Co-authored with Amanda Schreyer.

This whitepaper outlines seven core capabilities that turn static data into dynamic intelligence: data and content curation, AI-enhanced search and retrieval, intelligent matching and recommendation, context-aware data entry, AI-driven process automation, continuous learning and governance, and amplifying talent. It is written for leaders who want to scale AI responsibly without chasing models for their own sake.
