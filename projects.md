# Projects I Have Worked On

These are selected examples of the kinds of systems I have built and maintained over the years.

- Timesheet application for a major European-based consulting firm. Captured and reconciled time reporting across deployed consultants.
- Timesheet application for a major South African bank. Captured time and attendance for 80+ business analysts.
- Job tracking system for electronics repair workshops. Managed equipment goods receiving, repair tracking, spares, despatch and collections, invoicing, and reporting.
- Innovation hub solution for platinum mine cost saving management. Supported a connected environment where synchronisation across multi-user systems was a real challenge in the 1990s and early 2000s.
- Electronic Flight Bag for a Boeing 737-800. Delivered take-off and landing calculations for airports across Southern Africa, with training material, NOTAMs, and weather integration. Also presented it on a Microsoft DevDays roadshow to show early WPF adoption.
- Australia tax compliance system from the ground up. Built a greenfield system that supported the required compliance workflow and delivery needs.

These projects show the pattern that runs through most of my work: practical systems, real operational constraints, and delivery-focused engineering that solves the problem without unnecessary ceremony.
