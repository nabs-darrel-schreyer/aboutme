# Work history

My approach to my work is to remain an individual contributor while enabling those around me and driving delivery.

- Financial services and fintech. Delivery, secure platforms, APIs, and systems that had to be fast, reliable, and compliant.
- Retail and enterprise operations. Multi-cloud engineering frameworks, developer experience, and delivery at scale.
- Security and product engineering. Technical leadership in an environment where stability, clarity, and pragmatic engineering mattered.
- Travel and aviation. High-volume operational systems and customer-facing workflows.
- Government and public-sector systems. Software shaped by real operational constraints and practical delivery.
- Entrepreneurship, consulting, and digital product work. Architecture, hands-on delivery, and client-facing problem solving.

Most of my roles have blended hands-on engineering with people leadership or mentoring, technical leadership, architecture, and delivery process.
